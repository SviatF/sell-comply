# SellComply — Organic Growth Roadmap

Primary objective: build a genuinely useful product-compliance search product with the potential to grow from the first indexed clusters to 100k–300k+ monthly organic visits.

Traffic targets are goals, not guarantees. Scaling decisions must be based on Search Console evidence.

### 7. Product taxonomy expansion

Scale only with search demand:
8 → 30 → 50 → 100 → 200 product categories.

### 8. GEO expansion

6 markets → priority EU markets → 20–40 high-value markets.

### 9. Marketplace clusters

Amazon / Etsy / eBay / TikTok Shop / Shopify → additional platforms from demand data.

### 10. Regulation clusters

CE / GPSR / RED / RoHS / WEEE / Batteries / FCC / UK product rules / toy safety / cosmetics and additional high-intent regulations.

### 11. Question / long-tail engine

Generate only pages with a distinct, useful answer and sufficient regulatory data.

### 12. Comparison / decision pages

Build pages around real search intent, not generic filler.

### 13. Internal linking engine

Product ↔ market ↔ regulation ↔ marketplace ↔ checker.

### 14. Freshness engine

Last verified / effective date / official-source change / human review.

### 15. SEO quality gate

No indexable page ships without:
- distinct search intent
- unique useful data
- official sources
- internal links
- correct canonical
- useful CTA
- no hallucinated requirement claims
